# Style

Not written yet. Before building, replace each prompt below with decisions that follow from the
request, then set `src/style.ts` to match. Be specific: "dusty coral #e8836b" not "warm colors".

## Pitch
One sentence: what the player does and how it should feel.

## Pillars
Three words the game must be (e.g. chunky, sunlit, playful). Use them to settle every later choice.

## Touchstones
One or two games, films or art styles this looks and feels like, and what exactly to take from each.

## Palette
Four to six colors with names and hex values: sky, ground, the player, hazards, pickups, UI ink.
The player and anything dangerous must stand out from the world.

## Light and post
Lighting preset and time of day, fog, and which one or two post effects carry the look (bloom for
neon, grain and vignette for gritty, none for clean).

## Shapes and materials
How things are built: faceted low-poly, rounded toy-like, flat toon bands, glossy plastic. One
material family for everything.

## Camera
Angle, distance and how it moves: close and chasing, high and steady, fixed isometric.

## Feel
How heavy and fast things are, and what happens on the big moments (landing, hitting, scoring,
dying): which mix of squash, particles, shake, hit-stop and slow motion, and how strong.

## Sound
The mood (soft and round, bright and arcade, low and tense), and which sounds matter most.

## Type and UI
Display and body fonts, and how menus and the HUD look and move.
